# Business Model: Independent Financial & Investment Advisory Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-2412`
- ISCO-08: `2412`
- Occupation: Financial and Investment Advisers
- Social impact: financial-transparency, consumer-protection, local-jobs

## Customer

- individual investors
- small-business owners
- retirees

## Offer

- investment recommendation
- risk-profile assessment
- portfolio review
- disclosure packet preparation

## Revenue

- advisory fee (flat or AUM-based)
- financial-plan preparation fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no trade execution or fund transfer without governor gate
- suitability/risk-disclosure required before any investment recommendation is disclosed
- client financial records are auditable, not editable
